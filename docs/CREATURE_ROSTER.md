## Elemental Interactions

### Initial Elemental Chart

**Fire** → Strong against Nature, Weak against Water
**Water** → Strong against Fire, Weak against Lightning
**Earth** → Strong against Lightning, Weak against Nature
**Wind** → Strong against Earth, Weak against Fire
**Nature** → Strong against Water, Weak against Fire
**Lightning** → Strong against Water, Weak against Earth
**Light** → Strong against Shadow, Weak against Arcane
**Shadow** → Strong against Light, Weak against Nature
**Arcane** → Neutral interactions, special effects

*Note: This is a preliminary chart and subject to balancing.*

## Creature Roles

### Attacker
- High Attack stat
- Offensive abilities
- Focus on damage output

### Defense
- High Defense/Resistance stats
- Defensive abilities
- Focus on survivability

### Support
- High Magic stat
- Support abilities
- Focus on team enhancement

### Speed
- High Speed stat
- Priority abilities
- Focus on first strikes

### Specialist
- Unique abilities
- Specific element mastery
- Focus on special effects
