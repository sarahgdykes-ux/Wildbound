# Region Designs

This document provides detailed design specifications for each region in the Wildbound world.

## Verdant Hollow

**Theme:** Forest
**Main Identity:** Starting region and first Wildbond mystery
**Status:** Included in initial playable milestone

### Overview

Verdant Hollow is the player's starting region. Its environment should initially feel welcoming, gradually revealing increasingly mysterious locations and events. The region serves as an introduction to the game's core mechanics while establishing the central mystery of the failing Wildbond.

### Areas

#### Area 1: Bramblewick Village

**Purpose:**
- Introduce the player
- Establish the main story
- Provide shops and NPC interactions
- Introduce the sanctuary
- Host the first rival encounter

**Layout:**
- Central village square with fountain
- Player home in quiet corner
- Sanctuary entrance near village edge
- Shop building with various supplies
- Training grounds with battle arena
- NPC homes and quest locations
- Regional challenge board near square

**Key Locations:**
- **Player Home** - Starting location, safe resting place
- **Sanctuary Entrance** - Gateway to creature sanctuary
- **General Store** - Purchase items, taming supplies
- **Training Grounds** - Practice battles, creature training
- **Village Elder's Home** - Story introduction, quest giver
- **Challenge Board** - View and accept regional challenges

**NPCs:**
- Village Guide - Introduces player to mechanics
- Shopkeeper - Sells items and supplies
- Trainer - Offers battle practice
- Village Elder - Provides story context
- Rival - First competitive encounter

**Environmental Features:**
- Cobblestone paths
- Flowering gardens
- Wooden buildings
- Ambient village sounds
- Day/night cycle visible

**Creature Encounters:**
- Domesticated creatures in homes
- Wild creatures near village edge
- No aggressive encounters in village proper

**Progression Triggers:**
- Speak to Village Elder
- Receive first companion
- Visit sanctuary
- Learn basic controls
- Enter Whispering Woods

---

#### Area 2: Whispering Woods

**Purpose:**
- Introduce exploration
- Teach creature encounters
- Introduce different taming methods
- Provide early wild battles

**Layout:**
- Forest paths with branching routes
- Clearings with creature spawns
- Hidden areas off main paths
- Environmental obstacles
- Resource collection points

**Key Locations:**
- **Main Forest Path** - Primary route through woods
- **Hidden Clearing** - Secret creature encounter
- **Berry Bushes** - Taming item source
- **Old Tree** - Landmark, potential puzzle
- **Stream Crossing** - Environmental obstacle
- **Forest Edge** - Transition to other areas

**Environmental Features:**
- Tall trees with canopies
- Dappled sunlight
- Forest floor with plants
- Small streams and ponds
- Fallen logs as obstacles
- Ambient forest sounds

**Creature Encounters:**
- Mosskit (starter area)
- Shroomble (common)
- Voltkit (during storms)
- Wispin (near dark areas)
- Duskit (in shaded clearings)

**Taming Opportunities:**
- Trust-based taming (Mosskit)
- Discovery-based taming (Shroomble)
- Quest-based taming (Wispin, Duskit)

**Environmental Obstacles:**
- Fallen logs (can be moved with strength)
- Berry bushes (can be harvested)
- Streams (can be crossed with water creature)

**Hidden Secrets:**
- Secret clearing with rare items
- Ancient tree markings
- Underground passage (locked until later)

**Progression Triggers:**
- Tame first wild creature
- Win first wild battle
- Complete first NPC quest
- Discover secret location

#### Area 3: Stoneback Ridge

**Purpose:**
- Introduce environmental exploration abilities
- Provide more difficult encounters
- Introduce mineral-based creature habitats

**Layout:**
- Rocky pathways and cliffs
- Cave entrances
- Mineral deposits
- Elevated areas
- Breakable walls

**Key Locations:**
- **Ridge Path** - Main route through area
- **Crystal Cave** - Mineral deposit location
- **Breakable Wall** - Blocks hidden passage
- **Elevated Platform** - Requires flight or climbing
- **Mineral Vein** - Resource collection
- **Deep Cave** - Optional challenge area

**Environmental Features:**
- Rocky terrain
- Cave entrances
- Glowing minerals
- Stalactites and stalagmites
- Echoing cave sounds
- Dim lighting in caves

**Creature Encounters:**
- Pebblit (common)
- Elderbloom (rare, in specific grove)
- Rock-based creatures
- Cave-dwelling creatures

**Taming Opportunities:**
- Discovery-based taming (Pebblit)
- Environmental taming (Elderbloom)

**Exploration Abilities Required:**
- Rock breaking (for breakable walls)
- Illumination (for dark caves)
- Strength (for moving boulders)

**Environmental Obstacles:**
- Breakable walls (require rock breaking)
- Dark caves (require illumination)
- Boulders (require strength)
- Gaps (require flight or jump)

**Hidden Secrets:**
- Hidden mineral deposit
- Ancient cave paintings
- Secret passage to later area

**Progression Triggers:**
- Obtain rock-breaking ability
- Break first wall
- Enter deep cave
- Discover Elderbloom

#### Area 4: Moonwater Glade

**Purpose:**
- Introduce time-dependent encounters
- Reward nighttime exploration
- Introduce rare creatures

**Layout:**
- Magical lake at center
- Glowing plant life
- Hidden pathways
- Time-activated locations
- Puzzle areas

**Key Locations:**
- **Moonwater Lake** - Central feature, reflects moon
- **Glowing Grove** - Nighttime-only location
- **Hidden Path** - Revealed at night
- **Ancient Shrine** - Puzzle location
- **Starfall Point** - Special event location
- **Moonlit Clearing** - Rare creature spawn

**Environmental Features:**
- Magical lake with glowing water
- Bioluminescent plants
- Starlight visibility
- Day/night cycle crucial
- Ethereal atmosphere
- Magical sounds

**Creature Encounters:**
- Moondrift (rare, nighttime only)
- Light/Wind creatures
- Time-dependent spawns
- Mystery creatures

**Taming Opportunities:**
- Discovery-based taming (Moondrift)
- Time-based taming (night creatures)

**Time Mechanics:**
- Day version: Normal creatures, safe exploration
- Night version: Rare creatures, hidden paths, special puzzles
- Time transition: Visual and audio changes

**Environmental Obstacles:**
- Night-locked paths
- Time-activated puzzles
- Moonlight-dependent mechanisms

**Hidden Secrets:**
- Nighttime-only pathway
- Ancient constellation puzzle
- Hidden moon crystal
- Secret transformation location

**Progression Triggers:**
- Visit at night
- Discover Moondrift
- Solve constellation puzzle
- Unlock time-dependent mechanics

- Ancient stone architecture
- Magical symbols and runes
- Echoing acoustics
- Mysterious lighting
- Ancient power remnants
- Ominous atmosphere

**Creature Encounters:**
- Echolet (story-related)
- Hollow Guardian (boss)
- Ancient guardian creatures
- Magical constructs

**Taming Opportunities:**
- Discovery-based taming (Echolet)
- Story progression taming

**Puzzle Mechanics:**
- Sound-based puzzle system
- Symbol matching
- Pattern recognition
- Environmental interaction
- Cooperative puzzle elements

**Boss Encounter:**
- Hollow Guardian (multi-phase boss)
- Environmental hazards
- Guardian restoration event
- Story revelation after battle

**Story Elements:**
- Ancient Wildbond history
- Guardian's purpose
- Source of disruption
- Clue to next chapter

**Progression Triggers:**
- Enter ruins
- Solve sound puzzle
- Encounter Echolet
- Defeat Hollow Guardian
- Restore guardian's connection
- Discover Wildbond clue

## Region Progression

### Recommended Order (Full Game)
1. Verdant Hollow (starting region)
2. Emberfall
3. Drowned Kingdom
4. Frostveil
5. Sunscar Desert
6. Skyreach Isles
7. Shadowfen
8. Forgotten Expanse (endgame)

### MVP Progression
1. Bramblewick Village
2. Whispering Woods
3. Stoneback Ridge / Moonwater Glade (flexible order)
4. The Hollow Ruins

### Region Completion Criteria

Each region should include:
- [ ] Central settlement or landmark
- [ ] Multiple explorable environments
- [ ] Region-specific creatures
- [ ] Hidden locations
- [ ] Tamer challenges
- [ ] Significant story discovery
- [ ] Regional guardian or major encounter

## Environmental Mechanics

### Time of Day
- Day/Night cycle affects encounters
- Certain creatures only appear at specific times
- Time-locked puzzles and paths
- Visual and audio changes

### Weather
- Rain affects water abilities
- Storms attract lightning creatures
- Clear weather for normal exploration
- Special weather events

### Seasons (Future)
- Seasonal creature migrations
- Seasonal environmental changes
- Season-specific events
- Weather variety increases

## Notes

- Region designs are subject to change during development
- MVP focuses on Verdant Hollow only
- Future regions will be developed after core systems are proven
- Environmental mechanics will be introduced gradually
- Regional challenges will provide optional content
